Web Platform Field Guide

Everything on this page is plain HTML and CSS, with a little JavaScript. It is a checklist of what a page can show: strong, emphasis, marked text, inline code, Ctrl+C, CSS, H2O, E = mc2, removed and inserted text, and a link to MDN.

In case of conflict, consider users over authors over implementors over specifiers over theoretical purity.
