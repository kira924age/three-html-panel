Notes on lighthouses

A long page that scrolls, with what such pages often keep in view: a header that sticks to the top, a table of contents beside the text, a bar showing how far you have read, and a notice at the bottom.

Light Height Range

Lamp

The light itself: oil, then gas, then electric, behind the lens.

Kept clean and trimmed by the keepers through the night.

Lens

Rings of glass prisms that bend the lamp's light into a beam.

The largest stand taller than the keepers who polished them.

Range

How far out at sea the light is seen, in nautical miles.

It depends on the height of the light and on the weather.
